What should I send?

You don't need
a special occasion.

A few pictures from your day are welcome. Older photos are just as good.

Everyday life

The kids, the pets, dinner together, a project, a visit. No need to wait for a holiday or a perfectly posed picture.

A memory worth revisiting

Old family photos, holidays, reunions, and favorite trips. Scanned prints are welcome, too.

The original photo

Attach the original file rather than a screenshot or a small preview. It will hold up better on a TV.

Something for her room

Keep it family-friendly. Only send photos you're comfortable having displayed in Grandma's room.

The story behind McPics

Made for Grandma.
Shared by all of us.

McCormick Family Pictures was created with love for Grandma.

Grandma has always been such an important part of my life, and this project was built from a place of love and gratitude. The idea is simple: to give family an easy way to share everyday moments, old memories, and familiar faces with her.

Photos sent here can show up right on her TV, bringing a little more connection, comfort, and joy into her room throughout the day.

It is a small project, but it means a lot — a way to share with Grandma the people, memories, and everyday moments that make our family special.

Project updates

What's new

What's changed on the website and on Grandma's slideshow.

September 11, 2026A fresh look for the website

I refreshed the website so the photo-sharing instructions and the story behind McPics are easier to find.

  • Grandma's photo and the family story have more room, with a layout that works on phones and computers.
  • The send-photo buttons, copy-address option, and full-size photo reminder make getting started easier.
  • For anyone curious about the project, Behind the slideshow explains how it works and what took some figuring out.
July 6, 2026Quiet hours for the screen

The screen can now turn off on a schedule and return to the slideshow afterward. The computer stays on to collect new photos, without keeping the room bright overnight.

July 2, 2026More variety in the slideshow

I adjusted the photo rotation after finding that some pictures repeated too often.

  • Photos that haven't appeared recently get more priority, while new arrivals still get a short turn in the spotlight. The order isn't a strict loop.
  • Contributor names display more consistently, regardless of capitalization in the original email.
  • When more than one person sends the same picture, duplicate handling does a better job of keeping contributor credit without repeating the photo.
Earlier updates June–July 2026 · 8 updates
July 1, 2026Keeping contributor counts accurate

Two fixes help keep track of everyone's photos:

  • Incomplete sender information could make one person appear as two contributors. Those records are now handled more consistently.
  • Similar pictures taken moments apart were sometimes mistaken for duplicates. Stricter matching helps keep those separate shots in the slideshow.
June 26, 2026Replies when photos arrive

McPics can now send automatic replies when that option is enabled:

  • A thank-you confirms an import, with photos sent close together grouped into one reply.
  • If a photo is too small or an email has no photos attached, a notice explains how to try again.
  • Low-resolution photos are kept out of the rotation. Sending the original gives McPics a better version to use.
  • The McPics logo also gained a small heart above the i.
June 25, 2026Smoother photos and captions

This round of changes focused on watching the slideshow:

  • Photos fade into one another, captions wait for the photo to load, and the mouse pointer hides when idle.
  • The layout scales for Full HD and 4K screens. If an image fails to load, the slideshow can skip it and move on.
  • The Family Update board handles contributors with multiple email addresses more consistently, and cleanup helps retain higher-quality copies.
June 24, 2026Easier support from a distance

I added desktop shortcuts for the slideshow, settings, Family Update preview, and status checks. They make it quicker to check imports and the photo library, or reopen the slideshow without waiting through its normal startup delay.

June 10, 2026More reliable photo importing

McPics now recognizes exact duplicate photos from the same sender and can preserve credit when different people send the same picture. If the email service reaches a limit, importing stops cleanly and can try again later.

June 8, 2026A Family Update screen

The slideshow gained a Family Update screen showing recent photo activity.

  • Photo selection began giving new and rarely seen pictures more priority.
  • HEIC/HEIF photos from iPhones can be converted for the slideshow.
  • Installation tasks were separated from everyday startup so the computer has less to do when it turns on.
June 7, 2026Photo history and settings

McPics began recording which photos had appeared and how often, then using that history to choose the next picture. A local settings page made it possible to check and adjust the slideshow from the computer.

June 4, 2026The first slideshow

I built the first local photo slideshow for Grandma's TV. It read pictures from the computer and displayed them fullscreen in a browser.

A database kept track of photos and senders, folders organized the imports, and basic logs and health checks helped me support it remotely.
